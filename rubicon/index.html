About Rubicon Energy

Rubicon Energy owns and operates a diversified fleet of solar, wind, and grid-scale battery storage projects, delivering firm capacity to utilities and large commercial offtakers. The company is headquartered in Denver, Colorado, and reports its governance activities through this portal for transparency with shareholders.
